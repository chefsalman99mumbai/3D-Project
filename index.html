The Alibaug House

Private Residence · Alibaug, Maharashtra

A weekend residence on the Konkan coast, built from board-formed concrete, reclaimed teak, and glass that dissolves into the sea. The brief was edited to three materials and one instruction: let the light do the rest.

Malabar Hill Residence

Private Penthouse · Mumbai

A 6,000 sq. ft. sky residence for a family who wanted their home to feel like the last quiet room in the city. Smoked oak, hand-troweled lime plaster, and a floating brass stair hold the palette in shadow and warmth.
