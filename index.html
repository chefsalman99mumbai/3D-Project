The Alibaug House

Private Residence · Alibaug, Maharashtra

A weekend residence on the Konkan coast, built from board-formed concrete, reclaimed teak, and glass that dissolves into the sea. The brief was edited to three materials and one instruction: let the light do the rest.

Malabar Hill Residence

Private Penthouse · Mumbai

A 6,000 sq. ft. sky residence for a family who wanted their home to feel like the last quiet room in the city. Smoked oak, hand-troweled lime plaster, and a floating brass stair hold the palette in shadow and warmth.

The Solace Hotel

Boutique Hospitality · Goa, 42 Keys

A 42-key hideaway for a developer who refused to build “another beach hotel.” Every key, corridor, and courtyard was choreographed as a single sequence — arrival to breakfast to sea.

02 The Studio

A practice of restraint

Founded in Mumbai and now working across Delhi and Dubai, Atelier Noir is a small studio of architects and designers who believe that luxury is not abundance — it is clarity. We take on a limited number of commissions each year so that every project receives the same intensity of attention, from the first material sample to the last piece of hardware.

Our work is defined by a disciplined palette, an almost forensic study of light, and a refusal to decorate. Spaces are composed rather than filled. Surfaces are allowed to age. Light is treated as a primary material.
